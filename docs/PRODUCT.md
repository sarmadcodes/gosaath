# GoSaath — Product Brief

## Core idea
"Set up your commute once. GoSaath handles the rest."

GoSaath is not a ride-hailing app. It is a recurring-commute management product for
people who travel the same route regularly — to university or work — and want to share
genuine travel costs with verified people going the same way.

## Market
Initial market: Pakistan (Karachi-first content: DHA, Clifton, Gulshan, North Nazimabad,
PECHS, NED area, IBA area). Currency: PKR ("Rs. 200", "estimated contribution").

## Target users
- University students
- Office employees
- Private-sector employees
- Government employees

## Product pillars
1. **Recurring commute is the core object**, not a one-off ride search. A commute
   (e.g. "DHA → IBA, Mon–Fri, 7:30 AM") persists and GoSaath manages recurring
   passengers/drivers, attendance, confirmations, cancellations, skipped days,
   replacement drivers, notifications, safety, and ride history around it.
2. **Same user can be driver and passenger** on different days.
3. **Cost sharing, not commercial fares.** Always "estimated contribution" /
   "shared travel cost" / "fuel contribution" — never "fare" or "driver earnings".
4. **Trust is structural**: identity, university/work, driver, and vehicle verification;
   women-only preferences; SOS, trip sharing, reporting, blocking.
5. **Useful without daily app opens** — the commute dashboard, notifications, and
   replacement-driver flow are designed to run the routine even when the user isn't
   actively searching that day.

## What this is not
Not a taxi clone, not a social feed, not gamified. No emojis, gradients, glassmorphism,
neon, confetti, or decorative illustration in the UI. Premium through restraint:
typography, spacing, hierarchy, alignment, and a single controlled brand accent.

## Every screen answers
WHERE (route) · WHEN (schedule) · WHO (companions + verification) · TRUST (verification
status) · COST (contribution) · ACTION (clear next step).
